Privacy Policy


[Internet / Telemetry]
The app can retrieve alert data from the user's currently selected regions. Regions can separately be enabled or disabled individually by the user in the app's Region Settings.

The app may occasionally send statistics information to ORION, containing the current app state (data such as, but not limited to: alerts received, alerts forwarded to Discord, network error rates), and the current username (in Windows).

The app may also send errors and crashes, to ORION, containing the current app state (data such as, but not limited to: alerts received, alerts forwarded to Discord, network error rates), the current username (in Windows), and the error/crash data itself.

By default, the app will send statistics, errors, and crashes, to ORION. The information received is only visible to developers. You can opt out of all telemetry in the app's settings. To do this, open the app's settings. Click the "Advanced Mode" button (if you are in "Simple Mode"), then click the "Extra Options" button. Finally, uncheck the "Allow telemetry" check box.

[Discord Rich Presence]
The app connects to an open Discord client to provide activity data to Discord. The activity data will appear on the user's profile if they have allowed it in the app's settings. This activity data includes alert information, such as, the alert type, alert beginning/end date and time, locations specified by the alert, and partial alert text. Activity data is sent using the most recent alert that has been relayed, and displays for up to 15 minutes, or until the alert expires, whichever one comes first.

[Discord Webhooks]
The app can automatically send messages to a Discord text channel using a Discord webhook. Each alert message includes alert information, such as, the alert type, the alert severity, alert beginning/end date and time, locations specified by the alert, and full alert text. Messages are also sent when the app changes in status, such as SharpAlert starting, or SharpAlert exiting. If the computer running the app has a battery installed, the app will send customizable battery status messages.

[Storage]
The user's settings are stored locally on the device. The app stores user preferences and settings in the user's home directory, under "C:\Users\<username>\AppData\Local\SharpAlert". ("<username>" is a placeholder for the actual username!)

[Location]
The user can select locations to store locally in multiple forms. The user's selected locations are used in the alert processing parts of the app to determine if an alert should or should not be relayed.

ORION is a server operated and maintained by MaybeConnor, a contributing developer of SharpAlert. For questions on the privacy policy, or to request deletions, contact BunnyTub. Find contact information in the footer of this page.
