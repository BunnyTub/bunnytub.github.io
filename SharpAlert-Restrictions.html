Why am I restricted?

You can be restricted for a variety of reasons (see Restriction System), but here are common reasons on why you might've been restricted.

  • You are currently banned from EAS Culture on Discord.
  • You are currently muted or banned in r/EmergencyAlertSystem on Reddit.


What restriction types are there?

Depending on why you might have been restricted, there are different levels that can be assigned to one.

  • A severity of "1" or "Rich Presence restricted" means that you cannot use the Discord Rich Presence feature to display alert information on your Discord profile.
  • A severity of "2" or "Rich Presence and Webhook restricted" means that you cannot use the Discord Rich Presence feature or send messages to Discord Webhooks. This severity has been deprecated, and is no longer in use.
  • A severity of "100" or "client restricted" means that you cannot use the Discord Rich Presence feature to display alert information on your Discord profile, and SharpAlert will have an added delay on startup.


How does SharpAlert know if I'm restricted?

When SharpAlert connects to your Discord app for Rich Presence support, it will download a list of restricted users, then compare user IDs in the list, with the user ID retrieved from the Rich Presence data provided by Discord.
